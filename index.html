Popular questions

How does auction bidding work?

Place a refundable deposit, set your max bid on a lot, and we bid on your behalf at the Japanese auction. If you win, the deposit applies to your order; if not, it's released back to your wallet.

What is Total CIF pricing?

CIF = Cost + Insurance + Freight to your destination port. The price you see already includes ocean freight and marine insurance — no surprise shipping fees.

How long does shipping take?

Typically 3–6 weeks depending on destination port and sailing schedule. You can track every leg live under Shipment tracking.
